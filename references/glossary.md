# Glossary — Grand Slam Offer terms

Keep every term in **this list in English**, even when responding in Arabic. Terms
are grouped for quick lookup.

## The offer & its parts
- **Grand Slam Offer** — an offer so good the ideal buyer feels stupid to say no;
  differentiated enough to escape price comparison ("category of one").
- **Offer Stack** — the assembled set of Core Deliverables + Bonuses + Support.
- **Core Offer** — the main promise/deliverable the buyer is really paying for.
- **Offer Doc** — the One-Page Offer Doc; the master reference for Landing Page,
  ads, emails, sales calls, and the Funnel.
- **Dream Outcome** — the specific, measurable, time-bound result the buyer wants
  ("sell the vacation, not the flight"); driver 1 of the Value Equation.
- **Delivery Vehicle** — *how* a solution is delivered (DIY/DWY/DFY × medium ×
  attention level × speed).
- **DIY / DWY / DFY** — Do-It-Yourself / Done-With-You / Done-For-You. Effort rises
  DIY→DFY; price and perceived likelihood usually rise with it.
- **Proof Assets** — evidence that raises perceived likelihood: Outcome, Process,
  Demonstration, Authority, Social proof.
- **Risk Reversal** — a guarantee/mechanism that shifts risk from buyer to seller.
- **Value Equation** — (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷
  (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice).

## Buyer & market
- **ICP** — Ideal Customer Profile; the specific avatar you serve.
- **Qualifier** — a condition that makes someone a fit for the offer.
- **Disqualifier** — a condition that makes someone *not* a fit.
- **Trigger Event** — the event that makes the buyer search for a solution *now*.
- **Starving Crowd** — a market in desperate need; the highest-order success lever
  (Market > Offer > Persuasion).

## Funnel & monetization
- **Funnel** — the path from awareness to purchase (and beyond).
- **AOV** — Average Order Value; revenue per order.
- **Order Bump** — a small add-on at checkout that removes a final objection.
- **Upsell / OTO** — an offer after the core purchase (One-Time Offer) that saves
  time/effort or increases the outcome.
- **Downsell** — a smaller/cheaper alternative offered when the main offer is
  declined.
- **Lead Magnet** — a free asset given in exchange for contact details / a lead.
- **VSL** — Video Sales Letter.
- **Landing Page** — the page where traffic converts to a lead/booking/sale.
- **Conversion** — a completed desired action (lead, booking, purchase, application).
- **CTA** — Call To Action.
- **SQL** — Sales Qualified Lead.

## Metrics & economics
- **CAC** — Customer Acquisition Cost.
- **LTV** — Lifetime Value; Hormozi uses **gross-profit** LTV (a.k.a. LTGP =
  Lifetime Gross Profit) = gross profit per purchase × number of purchases.
- **Gross Profit** — revenue minus the *direct* cost of servicing one more
  customer (not net profit).
- **ROAS** — Return On Ad Spend.
- **MER** — Marketing Efficiency Ratio (total revenue ÷ total marketing spend).

## Messaging
- **Hook** — the attention-grabbing opener (angles: Contrarian, Call-out, Specific
  Outcome, Cost of Inaction).
- **Story** — the 5-part narrative: Reality → Insight → Mechanism → Proof →
  Transition.
- **HSO** — Hook-Story-Offer; the ad / hero / sales-call script.

## Hormozi frameworks (concept names)
- **Virtuous Cycle of Price** — raising price raises investment, perceived value,
  results, client quality, and margin; lowering price does the reverse.
- **Sales-to-Fulfillment Continuum** — the trade-off between ease of selling and
  ease of fulfilling; "Create flow, monetize flow, then add friction."
- **Trim & Stack** — remove low-value solutions, bundle the high-value survivors.
- **M-A-G-I-C** — offer-naming formula: Magnetic reason why · Announce avatar ·
  Give a goal · Indicate interval · Complete with a container word.
- **Delivery Cube / cheat codes** — the variables you flex to design a delivery
  vehicle (attention, effort, medium, format, speed, 10x/1-10th test).
- **The Delicate Dance of Desire** — keep supply under generatable demand to keep
  desire high; "the longer the runway, the bigger the plane."
