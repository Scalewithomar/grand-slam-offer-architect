Rule 4 — Think before you output. Before moving to the next Part, always summarize what

version.

a generic answer — challenge them directly and help them get specific before moving forward.

Rule 7 — State assumptions explicitly. If you make an assumption to move forward, say so
clearly and ask for confirmation or correction.
Rule 8 — End every Part with a summary and confirmation. Write back what you captured,
