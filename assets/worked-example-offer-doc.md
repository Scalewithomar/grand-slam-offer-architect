# ONE-PAGE OFFER DOC

## Offer Snapshot
- **Offer Name:** Back Sore No More — 6-Week Pain-Free Reset
- **Offer Level:** Core
- **Funnel Objective:** Booked Calls (free assessment)
- **Success KPI:** 12 new patients enrolled per month

## Who It's For
- **For:** Desk-bound professionals aged 35–55 with chronic lower-back pain that
  flares after sitting or lifting, who've tried painkillers and generic stretches
  without lasting relief.
- **Must have (Qualifiers):** pain persisting 3+ months · able to attend 2
  sessions/week for 6 weeks · cleared of red-flag conditions needing surgery.
- **Not for (Disqualifiers):** acute post-surgical cases · seeking a one-off
  massage only · unwilling to do at-home exercises.
- **Trigger Event:** a flare-up bad enough that they missed work or lost sleep.

## Dream Outcome
Help desk-bound professionals become pain-free enough to sit, lift, and sleep
normally within 6 weeks — without surgery, long-term painkillers, or giving up the
activities they love.

## Offer Stack
**Core Deliverables:**
1. **Root-Cause Movement Assessment** — 60-min diagnosis + written recovery plan
   (solves "I don't know what's actually wrong"). _$250 value_
2. **12 Guided Recovery Sessions** — 2×/week hands-on + supervised exercise
   (solves "I don't stick with it / do it wrong"). _$1,200 value_
3. **Pain-Free Desk Setup Protocol** — personalized ergonomic + micro-break plan
   (solves "my job re-injures me"). _$150 value_

**Bonuses:**
1. **"5-Minute Flare-Up Rescue" Video Library** — what to do the moment pain
   strikes (crushes "what if it comes back?"). _$99 value_
2. **Sleep-Right Positioning Guide** — cut night-time pain (solves the trigger).
   _$49 value_

**Support:** WhatsApp check-ins between sessions with a same-day reply on weekdays.

## Proof Plan
- **Already have:** 20+ Google reviews (social); before/after mobility scores from
  past patients (outcome); a documented assessment method (process).
- **Build this week:** 2 short patient video testimonials · a 90-second
  "how the assessment works" demonstration clip · a one-page mechanism explainer.

## Risk Reversal
**[Conditional] Service Guarantee:** attend all 12 sessions and do the at-home
protocol, and if you're not measurably more mobile and in less pain by week 6, we
keep treating you free until you are.

## Funnel Stack
- **Core:** Back Sore No More — 6-Week Pain-Free Reset
- **Order Bump:** Posture-Correcting Support Cushion — removes objection: "I'll
  undo my progress the moment I sit back down at work."
- **Upsell / OTO:** 3-Month Strength & Prevention Program — improves outcome:
  locks in results so the pain doesn't return.
- **Downsell:** Self-Guided Recovery Course + one assessment — smaller because it's
  DIY for budget-conscious or distant patients.

## Hook-Story-Offer
**Hook (Cost of Inaction):** "Every month you wait, that 'manageable' back pain is
teaching your body to move in the exact way that makes it worse."

**Story:**
- **Reality:** You've tried painkillers, YouTube stretches, maybe a massage — relief
  lasts a day, then the ache is back.
- **Insight:** Lower-back pain is rarely about the back — it's a movement pattern
  your body defaults to under strain.
- **Mechanism:** We find that pattern in one assessment, retrain it over 6 guided
  weeks, and fix the desk setup that re-triggers it.
- **Proof:** Patients average a measurable mobility gain by week 6 (see before/after
  scores + reviews).
- **Transition:** Start with a free assessment — we'll tell you exactly what's wrong
  and whether we can help before you commit to anything.

**Offer Close:**
- **For:** desk-bound professionals with 3+ months of lower-back pain.
- **Outcome:** sit, lift, and sleep pain-free in 6 weeks.
- **Deliverables:** root-cause assessment + 12 guided sessions + pain-free desk
  protocol (plus rescue video library & sleep guide).
- **Guarantee:** do the program and if you're not better by week 6, we treat you
  free until you are.
- **CTA:** Book your free Root-Cause Assessment this week — only 12 new patient
  spots per month.
